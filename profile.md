## Profile
Dr Kun Fu is a Reader in Innovation and Entrepreneurship at Loughborough University London. His research examines entrepreneurship, innovation, institutions, digital transformation and inclusive growth across developed and emerging economies.

## Positions
- **May 2025–present** — Reader in Innovation and Entrepreneurship, Institute for International Management and Entrepreneurship, Loughborough University London, UK.
- **Oct 2020–Apr 2025** — Senior Lecturer in Innovation and Entrepreneurship, Institute for Innovation and Entrepreneurship, Loughborough University London, UK.
- **Jul 2016–Sep 2020** — Lecturer in Innovation and Entrepreneurship, Institute for Innovation and Entrepreneurship, Loughborough University London, UK.
- **Mar 2012–Jul 2016** — Research Associate, Department of Innovation and Entrepreneurship, Imperial College Business School, London, UK.
- **Apr 2013–Feb 2016** — Research Fellow, Enterprise Research Centre, London, UK.
- **Aug 2012–Sep 2013** — External Consultant, Economics and Statistics Division, World Intellectual Property Organization, Geneva, Switzerland.

## Research Focus
- Innovation and entrepreneurship ecosystems
- Institutions, productive and informal entrepreneurship
- Digital transformation, business models and inclusive growth
- Equity crowdfunding, social capital and entrepreneurial finance
